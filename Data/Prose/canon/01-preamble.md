**Status: CANON.** The owner adopted it on 2026-11-21, and it replaces every earlier GridGame premise: "Under Morrow", "A War in Heaven" and the colour/moon outline. Every story text (crawls, conversations, lore, quests, barks, glossary) must agree with it. **Amended 2026-11-21 (GG-T577, owner):** the Somnolith is not a moon; the life in people is called **aether**; every hero is a present-day person except **the Warden**, the Last Sleeper; the Hierarch is the villain; and the story stays simple, because it is a video game.

**In one line:** an ancient thing hangs in the sky beside the Moon. Long ago it was stopped and left there, orbiting the world, and now it is coming closer. Everyone calls it **the Somnolith**. Its **aether** is also the life in every person. The Choir keeps it asleep with **the Lullaby**, a rite that takes people's aether to quiet it and keeps a share for the Choir's leader, **the Hierarch**. **The Warden**, the Last Sleeper, wakes 1,200 years too late and must fix what Halcyon left broken, with present-day people who join along the road. The tone is bittersweet, and the player chooses the ending.

**The sky.** There is one Moon, and there is the Somnolith:
- **The Moon** is the ordinary one: round, pale and familiar, always the same size in the sky. Nothing in the story happens to it.
- **The Somnolith is not a moon.** It is an ancient thing that was stopped on its way in and left hanging in the sky, where it orbits the world. It is a lumpy, potato-shaped mass of crusted, grown-looking stone, clustered blade-like crystals and bubbled deep blue and violet lumps flushed with red, with a faint light seeping out between them. Beside the constant Moon it is visibly larger in every chapter, because it is coming closer. The player measures time running out by how much of the sky it fills.

**The name is the clue.** *Somnolith* is an old word built from *somnus* (sleep) and *lithos* (stone). Everyone takes it as poetry for the strange still thing in the sky, "the sleeping stone". It is a literal description: a sleeper inside stone.
