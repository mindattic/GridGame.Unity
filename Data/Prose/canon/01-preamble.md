**Status: CANON.** The owner adopted it on 2026-11-21, and it replaces every earlier GridGame premise, including "A War in Heaven" and the colour/moon outline. It was read out of the art: the 213 portraits and 81 region backdrops. Every story text (crawls, conversations, lore, quests, barks, glossary) must agree with it. **Amended 2026-11-21 (GG-T575):** the world has two moons. The ordinary Moon is unchanged; the Somnolith is the second, misshapen one.

**In one line:** the world's second moon is alive. Everyone calls it **the Somnolith**, and its light is also the life in every person. It is coming down. The Choir keeps it asleep with **the Lullaby**, a rite that takes people's life to quiet it and keeps a share for the Choir. The heroes are sleepers woken from a failed vault, led by the First Sleeper, the last people whose light is wholly their own, crossing a world that has half given up. The tone is bittersweet, and the player chooses the ending.

**Two moons.** The sky holds two moons:
- **The Moon** is the ordinary one. It has always been there, round, pale and familiar, and it is always the same size in the sky. It is just a moon, and nothing in the story happens to it.
- **The Somnolith** is the second moon, and it is wrong. It is not round. It is a lumpy, potato-shaped mass like the small moons of Mars, a crusted, grown-looking stone of clustered blade-like crystals and bubbled amber-brown lumps, with a faint light seeping out between them. Beside the constant Moon it is visibly larger in every chapter, because it is drawing closer. The player measures time running out by how much of the sky it fills.

**The name is the clue.** *Somnolith* is an old word built from *somnus* (sleep) and *lithos* (stone). Everyone takes it as poetry for the strange, still second moon, "the sleeping stone". It is a literal description: a sleeper inside stone. The world has been saying the answer for ten thousand years.
