Family Wheel

One ring of all 69 heroes, ordered so neighbours play alike, cut into equal families. A hero standing on a cut belongs to both families. Move round the rim to pick up stat passives and step sideways into a neighbour family; move inward to go deeper into your own. Every move is reachable by every hero; each family border you cross adds to its price.

Which shape fits

Equal families need the ring to divide evenly, so each shape either adds open slots for new heroes or drops a few. Fit is the share of heroes who land in a family led by the family they have today.
