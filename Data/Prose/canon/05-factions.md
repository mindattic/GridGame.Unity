Every people in the world lives under the Moon and the Somnolith, and every one of them is defined by what it does with aether.

## The heroes

**Every hero is a present-day person, except the Warden.** They are people of this world as it is now: kids from the Underspire, hold squires, road-wardens, fen witches, sword-school students, pit fighters. They have paid the tithe like everyone else. What sets them apart is that they fight. Each hero is one single character named by role, "the Cleric", "the Thief" (see GG-ARC, Decisions). Recruiting and the Summon Circle are game systems with no special story meaning.

**The Warden is the Last Sleeper.** Halcyon's last guard, asleep in the Still on the night Halcyon fell, the Warden woke 1,200 years too late to a world that has forgotten Halcyon, and now must fix what Halcyon left broken. No one else came out of the Still. Because the Warden slept untouched by the tithe, the Warden's aether is wholly their own.

The heroes come from six peoples (factions.json):
- **The Street:** the Underspire's own, couriers, fighters, thieves and techwear runners who have dodged the Choir's tithe under Halcyon's ruins for generations.
- **The Free Holds:** the moor and hill holds, knights and militia bound by old oaths, who pay the Choir's tithe and hate it.
- **The Lantern Road:** the pilgrim road through the gorges, its sword-schools, monks and ninja keeping the lanterns lit and asking no questions.
- **The Fen-dark:** witches, healers and Choir runaways living among the grounded Arks, who know more about aether than they say.
- **The Ember:** red-and-black knights of the Shardfall who drink from its red rivers. Most of them want the Somnolith down; a few don't.
- **The Wild:** wild children, beast tamers and things the Bloom has touched, out where the Somnolith's fallen substance takes root.

## The world's powers

- **Halcyon (gone).** Halcyon stopped the Somnolith, sang the first Lullaby, and built the Anchors (taps), the Still (a sleep-vault) and the Arks (ships to escape the pull). It overdrew and fell 1,200 years ago. Its ruins are the Halcyon region: dead towers under a sky gone pink. Its ships lie inland in the Arkgrave.
- **The Still.** Halcyon's sleep-vault at the foot of the spire, under an oculus that frames the Somnolith, where the Warden slept. The Choir holds it now and fills its tanks with the tithe on its way up to the Cathedral.
- **The Choir and the Hierarch.** The heirs of the singers who kept the Lullaby going the night Halcyon fell, seated in the Choir's Cathedral at the top of Halcyon's spire.
  - **The tithe:** once a year the Choir's white collectors take aether from every hold, "the colour", and the Lullaby sings it up to keep the Somnolith asleep. Those who pay too much come back Hollow.
  - **The skim:** the Lullaby takes more than the Somnolith needs, and the surplus fills the Cathedral organ's pipes, the reservoir the Hierarch drinks from.
  - **The Hierarch** is the villain: the Choir's human leader, always "the Hierarch". Only the Hierarch knows the whole truth: what the Somnolith is, that the aether in people is its aether, and that the skim is why the sleep keeps thinning. Everyone below believes sincerely.
  - **Its forces:** the **Red Seal** (its tithe wardens), the **Bulwark** (its soldiers in white power armour) and the **Kiln Guard** (porcelain knights, Hollow soldiers kept as the Hierarch's honour guard). Its white robes and red crosses are the white-and-red law made into a uniform.
  - **The Captain:** the Free Holds' militia captain, who sold the holds' tithe to the Choir and hunts the runaways who stole its caravan.
- **The Underspire.** The neon city under Halcyon's ruins, home of the Street and of gangs like Redline. It still draws aether off Halcyon's old taps to keep its lights on, and so keeps pulling the Somnolith closer.
- **The Watch.** The Wraith Knights of the Watch: Halcyon's Anchor guard, still at their posts at every arch long after they should have died.
- **The Infernalists.** Ember cultists who want the Somnolith to come all the way down, and mean to open the Anchors wide to drag it.
- **The holds and the road.** The feudal present: castles, keeps and lantern villages that pay the Choir's tithe and live under a Somnolith they worship and fear without understanding it.

## What the monsters are

- **The Hollow:** people whose aether has been drawn away, by the blanking or by too much tithe: porcelain knights, white-sheeted crowds, mannequins, and Mocking Echoes, which can only turn others' moves back on them because they have nothing of their own. Red seeping through cracks means a little aether is still in there.
- **Fallen with the shell:** many-eyed white cyclopes, sandmaws and skelepedes, things that came down from the Somnolith's crust or out of it.
- **Halcyon's failures:** purple prototypes, cyber-zombies, heavy troopers, mecha armour and tech gremlins: attempts to put aether into bodies and machines.
- **Too much red:** hellspawn, infernalists and the Demon Lord, what grows where aether takes hold too fast, or where a body takes more than it can hold.
- **Natural:** wolves, bats, frogs, slimes, trolls, yetis and nagas, ordinary creatures of a world under strain. Tree golems are moss growing over old bone.
