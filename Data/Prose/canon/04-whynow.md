## Why it is coming now

1. **The draw never stopped.** Halcyon fell, but the Underspire still runs on moonlight. Its neon is the old taps, rewired by people who don't know or don't care what they are pulling on.
2. **The shell is breaking.** As Morrow sinks it sheds its debris. The Shardfall is where the first great pieces came down. Its colossal curved spikes are not all rock: some are Morrow's own body showing through, claw or rib or horn.
3. **It is waking.** Sleep thins as the drain deepens, and waking things are hungry. The coupling now runs the other way. This is **the blanking**: people near the lines, or under a close moon, have their colour and self drawn up into it. What is left is white: porcelain shells, white-sheeted crowds, and blank figures like Echo.
4. **Where its life lands, things grow.** The Bloom is where Morrow's substance fell and took root, red life in a white ruin. It is the first sign that what is coming is not only death.

**The visual law.** White means hollowed and red means alive. Heroes wear the loudest colours in the game because they still have their colour. Red bleeding through white, as in the porcelain knights' cracks and the Bloom's blossoms on white stone, means something alive is still inside.
