## Peoples and powers

- **Halcyon (gone).** Halcyon caught the sleeper and built three things: the Anchors (taps), the Still (a vault) and the Arks (evacuation ships). It overdrew, then collapsed under monstrous tides. Its last ships were stranded inland when the waters fell back, and their wreckage is the Arkgrave. Its ruins are the Halcyon region: towers under a sky gone pink.
- **The Still.** A vault where Halcyon froze its people as a last resort, under an oculus that frames the moon. The moonlight stored there kept the sleepers unchanged. **The heroes are Still sleepers**, which is why so many wear modern clothes in a medieval world. **The Summon Circle is literal:** each summon thaws another sleeper.
- **The Choir.** The descendants of Halcyon's lullaby-singers, centred on the Choir's Cathedral. They are not trying to bring the moon down. They are desperately trying to keep it asleep, by singing the old lullaby with words they no longer understand. **The tithe is people:** a person's colour is sent upward to quiet the sleeper. They are sincere and horrifying, and they are right that it is waking. Their white robes and red crosses are the white-and-red law made into a uniform.
- **The Underspire.** A neon undercity that still draws moonlight to keep its lights on, and so keeps pulling the moon down. Its gangs and techwear crews are the present-day overdraw.
- **The Free Holds, the Tithe Moors and the Lantern Road.** The feudal present: castles, keeps and lantern villages. They pay the Choir's tithe and live under a moon they worship and fear without understanding it.

## What the monsters are

- **Blanked people:** porcelain knights, white-sheeted undead crowds, mannequins, and Echo and Mocking Echo. The white is hollowness. Red seeping through cracks means someone is still in there.
- **Fallen with the shell:** many-eyed white cyclopes, sandmaws and skelepedes, things that came down from Morrow's shell or out of it.
- **Halcyon's failures:** purple prototypes, cyber-zombies, heavy troopers, mecha armour and tech gremlins. They are attempts to put moonlight into living bodies and machines.
- **Too much red:** hellspawn, infernalists and the demon lord. This is what grows where Morrow's life takes hold too fast.
- **Natural:** wolves, bats, frogs, slimes, trolls, yetis and nagas, ordinary creatures of a world under strain. Tree golems are moss growing over old bone.
