## Why it is coming now

**The Somnolith**, the world's misshapen second moon, has hung asleep beside the ordinary Moon for ten thousand years. Now it is coming down: anyone who looks up can see it larger than their grandparents saw it, while the Moon beside it stays exactly the same size. This entry covers why.

0. **The arrival is resuming.** The Somnolith was frozen mid-landing, not turned away. Every century of drawing, the pull and the thinning sleep let the old descent continue a little further. The approach is not a new disaster. It is the original arrival, ten thousand years late, and no one knows what it will do when it completes.
1. **The draw never stopped.** Halcyon fell, but the Underspire still runs on the Somnolith's light. Its neon is the old taps, rewired by people who don't know or don't care what they are pulling on.
2. **The Choir keeps more than it needs.** The Lullaby takes more life than the Somnolith's sleep requires, and the surplus stays in the Cathedral. A starved sleeper is a restless sleeper, so every year the Lullaby has to take more.
3. **The shell is breaking.** As the Somnolith sinks it sheds its crust. The Shardfall is where the first great pieces came down. Its colossal curved spikes are not all rock: some are the Somnolith's own body showing through, claw or rib or horn.
4. **It is waking, and its light is flowing home.** This is **the blanking**. People near the lines, under a close Somnolith, or tithed too hard have their thread drawn back up into it, and with it their colour and self. What is left is white: porcelain shells, white-sheeted crowds, and blank mannequin figures. It is not hunger, malice or will. It is like water finding its level: a stirring body draws its light back, and the threads come home.
5. **Where its life lands, things grow.** The Bloom is where the Somnolith's substance fell and took root, red life in a white ruin. It is the first sign that what is coming is not only death.

**The sign in the sky.** The two moons are the world's clock. The Moon is the fixed measure, always the same size; the Somnolith beside it swells chapter by chapter, its crystals and lumps plainer to the naked eye, its seeping light brighter, until in Morrow's Shadow it fills the sky.

**The visual law.** White means hollowed and red means alive. Heroes wear the loudest colours in the game because their light is full. Red bleeding through white, as in the porcelain knights' cracks and the Bloom's blossoms on white stone, means a thread of light is still inside.
