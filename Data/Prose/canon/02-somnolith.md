**The Somnolith** is the second of the world's two moons: a misshapen, crusted mass that hangs beside the ordinary Moon and grows larger in the sky as it draws closer. It is alive. This entry covers what it is, how it came to be in the sky, and the evidence the world has forgotten.

## The core truth

- **The world has two moons, and only one is a moon.** The Moon, round and pale, has always been there and never changes. The Somnolith, the second moon, is a living thing that came in from the dark about ten thousand years ago. It was small then, drifting and possibly wounded.
- **It was put to sleep.** The civilization that became Halcyon sang it into hibernation and held it in the sky.
- **Its shell is debris.** Over ten millennia it turned and slept while dust, ice and stray stone settled on it, layer on layer. The shell never rounded out the way a true moon does. It grew the way a mineral crust grows, in lumps and clusters.
- **What it looks like.** An irregular, potato-shaped body like the small moons of Mars, never a sphere. Up close, or through a good glass, its surface is grown rather than cratered: clusters of flat, blade-like crystals, bubbled amber-to-brown lumps like a kidney stone or a desert rose, glints, and pale fibrous tufts like frost or hair. A faint light leaks out of the gaps between the crystals, brightest when it stirs. It looks organic and mineral at once, and it looks wrong. Next to the familiar Moon, people find it unsettling to look at for long.
- **It is getting bigger.** The Moon is always the same size in the sky. The Somnolith is not: generation by generation, and now season by season, it fills more of the sky beside the Moon. That difference is how anyone can see it is coming closer.
- **Its name is a description.** The world calls it **the Somnolith** ("sleeping stone", from *somnus* + *lithos*) and believes the name is only poetry. It is a literal account of what it is: a sleeper wrapped in stone. It is always "the Somnolith" in full, never shortened or nicknamed.
- **The model** is a dormant living body wearing a shell of grown rock: something alive that the sky disguised.
- **It is a force of nature, not a character.** It never speaks, bargains or explains itself. It sleeps, stirs, draws and sheds the way a storm or a tide does. Every motive the player sees belongs to people. No text gives the Somnolith lines or a point of view.
- **No one knows what it is for, and the story never says.** It never arrived, so no one has seen what it does on arrival. It might be a predator, a protector, a seed, or something with no human word. Every character's certainty is a guess:
  - the Choir calls it Mother and God;
  - Halcyon's engineers called it a resource;
  - the Bloom-touched call it a gardener;
  - the blanked are taken as proof that it is a predator.

  The bible fixes what happened to it, never what it is for, and no text may settle the question.
- **Closing line:** *"We always called it a stone that sleeps. It was never a stone. It was a sleeper, and we buried it in the sky."*

## The history: arrival, freezing, feeding, fall, forgetting

1. **The arrival (about 10,000 years ago).** The Somnolith came down toward the world to do whatever its kind does. No one alive then knew what that was, and no one has learned since.
2. **The freezing.** Halcyon's ancestors stopped it in the last moment before landfall. Thousands sang the first Lullaby at once, froze the arrival mid-act, and held the Somnolith asleep in the sky beside the Moon. Whatever it came to do never happened. It was only paused. The singers acted out of fear of the unknown, not knowledge, and no one has ever been sure whether they saved the world or stopped something that would have saved it.
3. **The feeding.** Halcyon learned to draw on the frozen Somnolith's light and built a civilization on it, with towers, Anchors, the Still and the Arks. Over millennia the light ran so deep in them that it became part of them, woven into every life. Whatever the Somnolith is, a part of it now lives in everyone.
4. **The fall: the Undertow (1,200 years ago, owner-confirmed).** The heroes went into the Still around this time and slept for about twelve centuries.
   - **The cause:** the Somnolith's pull was already growing, and Halcyon tried to escape it. Lifting the Arks took more light than had ever been drawn at once. The spike broke the Somnolith's sleep for a single night.
   - **The night:** the coupling ran backwards and light surged home up every thread. Most of Halcyon was blanked by dawn. The tides rose higher than towers and threw the Arks inland, where they lie in the Arkgrave. The Anchors cracked, and the city fell.
5. **The forgetting.**
   - **The singers:** a remnant kept singing through the Undertow and put the Somnolith back under. They became the Choir.
   - **The dark archives:** Halcyon kept its records in archives powered by the Somnolith's light, and when the city went dark its memory went dark with it.
   - **What survived:** chants, half-understood rites, and a second moon to worship.
   - **What the Choir buried:** the rest, out of shame and out of fear of what people would do if they learned the thing in the sky is alive and inside them.

Over twelve centuries the Somnolith's true origin became myth, then nothing. The world now believes there have always been two moons.

## The forgotten clue

The record has a seam. Every painting, carving, poem and star chart older than about ten thousand years shows only one moon. None shows a second:

- **Before the seam:** old night skies hold the Moon alone, round and familiar. The oldest shoreline ruins carry only the Moon's gentle tide line.
- **At the seam:** in a single layer of the record a second, misshapen moon is suddenly everywhere, beside the first: in poetry, in calendars, and carved over doorways. The carvers drew the Moon as a clean circle and the newcomer as a lumpy, bristling shape, and from that layer on every sky has both. Shorelines from the same age gain a second, harsher tide line far above the first.
- **What changes in the figures:** before the seam, carved people are drawn flat and plain. After it, they are drawn with a glow at the chest, the first images of the inner light. People did not always carry the Somnolith's light. It began when the Somnolith came.

The party gathers this as relics across the campaign. The world has forgotten it, and the Choir does not want it remembered.

## The open mystery: predator, protector, or other

The evidence is real, but it never settles the question:
- **For predator:** the blanking, and the red hellspawn.
- **For protector or gardener:** the Bloom, life flourishing where its substance falls.
- **The debris itself:** for ten thousand years it has gathered stray stone out of the sky that might otherwise have fallen on the world. Was it catching that stone, or only collecting it?
- **What the blanking proves:** nothing. It is what happens when a stirring body draws back its own light, whatever that body is for.

Characters argue the question, factions are built on their answers, and the ending is a choice made without knowing. Writers keep the mystery open, including in the endings.
