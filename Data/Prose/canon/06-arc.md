## The campaign arc, in the game's chapter order

The moon is drawn larger in each region's backdrops as the chapters go on. The story follows that approach.

| Chapter | Region | Discovery |
|---|---|---|
| Prologue | The Tithe Moors | The heroes wake from the Still into a world paying a tithe to a moon. |
| 1 | The Lantern Road | A moonless mural in a shrine raises the first question: why does the oldest art have no moon? |
| 2 | The Arkgrave | Under the wrecks is a harbour with no tide line. Halcyon tried to flee, and something made the seas rise. |
| 3 | The Anchor Wastes | The arches are taps, not supports. The draw is the pull. |
| 4 | Halcyon | Ruins and records show that Halcyon caught Morrow, drank from it, and could not stop. |
| 5 | The Bloom | Morrow's life takes root here. It is not only a monster. |
| 6 | The Underspire | The active drain: someone is still pulling. |
| 7 | The Shardfall | The shell breaks and the body shows through. It is waking. |
| 8 | Morrow's Shadow | Under the moon, gravity fails and stone rises. It is close enough to speak. |

The Still and the Choir's Cathedral are story locations threaded through the arc: the Still as the heroes' origin, and the Cathedral as the Choir's seat. The postgame regions (Free Holds, Drowned Arks, Deep Shardfall, Under the Anchors) go back to the world after the ending and must agree with the ending chosen.

## Endings

None of the endings is clean:
- **Sing it back down.** Side with the Choir, keep the light, and keep paying the tithe. The world survives as it is, and keeps shrinking.
- **Cut the lines.** Sever every tap and the pull stops. Morrow drifts back up, and may wake or may leave. The world loses moonlight and its tides, and maybe the moon itself, and learns to live in the dark under an empty sky, as before the seam.
- **Wake it.** Meet Morrow as it was before the debris: something small, old and starving, caught ten thousand years ago.

## Open questions (owner to decide)

- Is there a fixed lead, or is the whole party the protagonist?
- What is the tone: hopeful, bleak, or bittersweet?
- Is one ending canon, or does the player choose?
- What does Morrow want when it speaks?
- Does any Choir leader know the truth?
- Echo, a blanked figure, could join the party and gain colour as the story goes on. Their portrait is deliberately blank until then.
