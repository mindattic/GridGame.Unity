## Questions

### Q1. Premise: how much does the player learn, and when?

The canon holds a lot (the bond, the skim, the blanking, the shell, the Bloom). The reveal order in 06-arc is: the
Manalith is not on the oldest art (Lantern Road), the shell and its body (Shardfall), the arches are taps (Anchor
Wastes), the Still's surplus, Halcyon's archive (the mana in everyone is the Manalith's), the Cathedral (the skim).
- A. Keep that order; the full bond is revealed at Halcyon (chapter 4), before the Hierarch.
- B. Hold the bond back until after the Hierarch falls, so the Cathedral is about the skim only and the postgame carries
  the bigger reveal.
- C. Reveal the bond early (Lantern Road) so the whole campaign is played knowing the trap.

### Q2. Factions: what part does each of the six peoples play?

Street, Free Holds, Lantern Road, Fen-dark, Ember, Wild are heroes' origins. Only the Ember have a stated stance (most
want the Manalith down; a few don't). The others have no stated stake in the endings.
- A. Each people backs one ending (for example Ember = let it come, Lantern Road = keep it asleep, Street/Fen-dark = cut
  the lines, Free Holds and Wild split) and their heroes comment on the final choice in party talks.
- B. Peoples stay flavour only; heroes disagree as individuals by role, not by faction.
- C. Only two or three peoples matter to the plot (Ember, Street, Free Holds); the rest stay flavour.
- Sub-question: do the Ember's few dissenters become a recruitable or boss-turned-ally beat?

### Q3. The crew

Today the prologue says "a handful of present-day people" steal the white tithe caravan; heroes are unnamed by role; the
caravan wagons have named crew NPCs (Brannoc, Sister Ilsabet, Old Hask, Penny Tallow, Wren and Dace, Lio, Osk) with
barks only. Recruits join at story beats (4th after Tithe Moors I, 5th at Shardfall, 6th at Anchor Wastes).
- A. Heroes stay role-only; the caravan crew are the only named voices and carry the warmth between fights.
- B. Promote a few caravan crew to speaking parts in crawls and the ending (they live or die by the ending chosen).
- C. Add the "bittersweet" losses to the crew: decide which, if any, can be lost mid-campaign (the game currently has no
  permadeath rule, so this needs a design check).
- Sub-questions: Does the Commander speak, or is the Commander a silent lead whom others address? Is the Herald (the winged
  Wild girl in the art) cut, or reused as a Wild hero/NPC with no Host link? Does the Captain survive the Free Holds
  (he still appears in Deep Shardfall)? Options for the Captain: killed at Tithe Moors III; spared and later an ally;
  returns as a postgame foe.

### Q4. The Hierarch

Canon: human, knows the whole truth, drinks the skim from the organ, falls at the Cathedral III (Undying rule; the first
win triggers the ending crawl "Still Here"). The class currently uses the mannequin art as a placeholder (GG-T351).
- A. A true believer: the skim is a necessary sacrifice and the Hierarch honestly thinks the Lullaby must go on. Offers
  the Warden a place.
- B. A hollowing tyrant: the Hierarch is partly Hollow already, kept alive by the organ, the cost made visible.
- C. A tragic figure tied to Halcyon's last guard (risks complexity; at odds with "keep it simple").
- Sub-questions: Is the Hierarch's death final or does the Choir name a successor? What does the boss say before the
  fight (one speech, with a choice for the player or none)? Does the Hierarch's portrait need new art first?

### Q5. The eight chapters and their order

Main campaign: Tithe Moors, Lantern Road, Shardfall, Arkgrave, Anchor Wastes, Still, Halcyon, Cathedral (the Hierarch).
Postgame: Bloom, Free Holds / Underspire / Drowned Arks, Deep Shardfall / Under the Anchors, Morrow's Shadow. The arc
table numbers crawls 1 to 8 over those, plus a prologue; the Shardfall, Still and Cathedral have no numbered crawl row.
- A. Keep as written: crawls 1-4 in the main campaign, 5-8 postgame, a prologue and one ending crawl.
- B. One crawl per region (more story, more Prose drafting, about 13 crawls).
- C. Fewer: collapse to a prologue, a midpoint (Halcyon) and the ending, and let party talks carry the rest.
- Sub-question: chapter 6 (Free Holds, Underspire, Drowned Arks) says everyone wants the empty spire; confirm the
  postgame is "the world after the Hierarch" and who the antagonists are before the Ember march.

### Q6. The ending, and one real contradiction to resolve

Two statements conflict: the arc says "the postgame regions reflect the choice", but the choice is made at the very end,
at Morrow's Shadow. The current ending crawl ("Still Here") plays on the first win over the Cathedral, before any choice.
- A. Move the choice earlier: after the Hierarch falls, the player picks the ending's direction, and the postgame regions
  (Bloom to Morrow's Shadow) play out that choice; Morrow's Shadow III is the payoff.
- B. Keep the choice last; postgame regions are the same for everyone and reflect only "the skim ended". The choice is a
  final dialogue at Morrow's Shadow III, with three ending crawls and a credits variant each.
- C. Make the first ending crawl itself the choice, and let postgame be an epilogue-style endless mode.
- Sub-questions: how much does the player see of each ending ("Let it come" must stay a mystery)? Is the choice
  reversible with New Game+ (the ending flag already resets per cycle)? Do the three choices need a visible cost in the
  party (a hero speaks against it, a crew member leaves)?
