Where heroes come from

Seven ways in. Only the two banners are random and only they take Shards; both also take Summon Tickets that play hands out. Other pages own their rows.

The rules

Banners

  • Standard is always open. Its pool is every summonable hero: the current Summon Circle draw pool plus every summon-only hero whose Featured run has ended. Each hero is equally likely.
  • Featured runs 14 days with 1 featured hero. 50% of its pulls bring that hero; the rest are a Standard pull.
  • A summon-only hero debuts Featured, then joins Standard for good. No hero is ever pulled from the game.
  • Featured banners rotate on a published calendar; the next one is shown a banner ahead. No countdown pressure inside the screen beyond the end date.

Rates and hard pity

  • Every pull is a hero. There are no rarity tiers and no filler: equal power leaves nothing to tier.
  • New-hero pity: the 10th pull in a row without a new hero brings one you don't have, while that banner has any. So a x10 always holds a new hero until the banner is complete.
  • Featured pity: the 5th Featured pull without the featured hero brings them. The counter carries to the next Featured banner.
  • Spark: every 25 pulls on a banner kind earn a pick of any hero on that banner. Unspent Sparks keep.
  • Odds are shown before every pull and computed by the same code that rolls (as `DiamondSummon.Odds` does today, GG-T503), pity included: when pity is due the odds list says so.

Duplicates: Echoes

  • A hero you already have arrives as an Echo: +1 SP for that hero to spend on the skill board, up to 5 Echoes (5 SP, about five levels of nodes).
  • Past the cap, a duplicate gives 2 Ticket fragments; 10 fragments become a Summon Ticket on their own.
  • An Echo never adds a stat the board can't give, so a fully Echoed hero is the same hero five levels sooner, not a stronger one.
  • Naming: "Echo" is already a Support wanderer and the Mocking Echo foe (Â§25.11.2). If the owner wants one meaning per word, "Resonance" is the fallback.

Paying: Tickets, Shards, x1 and x10

  • A pull costs 1 Summon Ticket or 100 Mana Shards ($1.00, or 600 G through the exchange, Â§25.18.1). Tickets are spent first; the confirm names the split.
  • x10 is ten pulls in one tap for exactly ten times the price: no discount and no bonus pull, so x1 is never the worse deal.
  • Tickets are earned, never sold: 10 on the first Summon Circle visit, 1 per campaign stage first cleared, 5 per chapter finished, 1 per 5 battles won, and fragments.
  • The summoning rides the Summon paywall job (1 s); a x10 reveals ten cards in one sequence, new heroes first-lit.

Pull history

  • The save keeps the last 500 pulls: time, banner, hero, New / Echo / fragments, what was paid (Tickets, Shards, gold exchanged) and both pity counters after it.
  • A History tab on the Summon screen lists them newest first and totals Shards and Tickets spent.
  • Real-money Shard purchases stay in the account wallet's ledger (Â§25.18.1); the history only says which pull spent them.

A free path to every hero

  • Every summon-only hero is reachable on Tickets earned in play: the campaign alone pays about 102 Tickets.
  • Pity bounds the worst case: one new hero per 10 pulls plus one Spark per 25. The check below fails if the summon-only roster outgrows the campaign's Tickets.
  • Featured heroes join Standard, and Sparks keep, so a missed banner costs nothing but time.
  • Bots and Test Mode saves never pull (as with the Diamond draw today).
