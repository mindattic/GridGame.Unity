## Why it is coming now

1. **The draw never stopped.** Halcyon fell, but the Underspire still runs on moonlight. Its neon is the old taps, rewired by people who don't know or don't care what they are pulling on.
2. **The Choir keeps more than it needs.** The Lullaby takes more life than the Pearl's sleep requires, and the surplus stays in the Cathedral. A starved sleeper is a restless sleeper, so every year the Lullaby has to take more.
3. **The shell is breaking.** As the Pearl sinks it sheds its debris. The Shardfall is where the first great pieces came down. Its colossal curved spikes are not all rock: some are the Pearl's own body showing through, claw or rib or horn.
4. **It is waking, and it is calling its light home.** This is **the blanking**. People near the lines, under a close Pearl, or tithed too hard have their thread pulled back up into it, and with it their colour and self. What is left is white: porcelain shells, white-sheeted crowds, and blank figures like Echo. To the Pearl this is not hunger. It is taking back what was always its own.
5. **Where its life lands, things grow.** The Bloom is where the Pearl's substance fell and took root, red life in a white ruin. It is the first sign that what is coming is not only death.

**The visual law.** White means hollowed and red means alive. Heroes wear the loudest colours in the game because their light is full. Red bleeding through white, as in the porcelain knights' cracks and the Bloom's blossoms on white stone, means a thread of light is still inside.
