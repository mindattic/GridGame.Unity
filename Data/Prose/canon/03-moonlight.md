## Moonlight: one mechanism, two vocabularies

**Science.** A sleeping body gives off energy as a living body gives off heat; Morrow gives it off by dreaming.
- **The taps:** Halcyon tapped that energy through the **Anchors**, the colossal arches of the Anchor Wastes.
- **The store:** it kept the energy in the cyan tanks of the **Still**. That cyan glow is liquid moonlight, and it is the same cyan as the Choir Cathedral's organ pipes.
- **The coupling:** energy cannot be drawn across a distance without one. Every tap is a line between the sleeper and the ground, and drawing power down a line also pulls along it.
- **The winch:** a little draw is a tug no one notices. Ten thousand years of rising demand is a winch. The draw became a physical pull: each century the orbit sinks, the tides worsen, and Morrow comes closer.

**Magic.** It is the same truth in older words: *what you feed on, feeds back.* Sympathy runs both ways.
- **The binding:** Halcyon's sorcerers bound Morrow with a lullaby, the first Choir song, and drank its dreams.
- **The reach:** a drained dreamer reaches toward whatever is draining it. In its sleep, Morrow turns toward the hand that bleeds it.

**Both are true.** Engineers wrote "coupling force" and priests wrote "sympathy", and both described the same lines of light. In play this means moonlight-powered things such as Halcyon tech, Choir hymns and Underspire neon are one system under two names. Magic and science never contradict each other in this world.
