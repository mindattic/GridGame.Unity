## The core truth

- **The moon is not a moon.** It is a living thing called **Morrow**: once small, it drifted in from the dark about ten thousand years ago, possibly wounded.
- **It was put to sleep.** The civilization that became Halcyon lulled it into hibernation and held it in the sky.
- **Its shell is debris.** Over ten millennia it turned and slept while dust, ice and stray stone collected around it, layer on layer, until it looked like a moon. Everyone alive believes it is one.
- **Its shadow is the last region.** The final region, Morrow's Shadow, is where its shadow falls as it comes down.
- **The model** is a dormant living body wearing a shell of rock: something alive that the sky disguised.

## The forgotten clue

The record has a seam. No painting, carving, song or star chart older than about ten thousand years shows a moon:

- **Before the seam:** old night skies are drawn empty, and the oldest shoreline ruins have no tide line, because there were no tides.
- **At the seam:** in a single layer of the record the moon is suddenly everywhere, in poetry, in calendars, and carved over doorways.

The party gathers this as relics across the campaign. The world has forgotten it, and the Choir does not want it remembered.
