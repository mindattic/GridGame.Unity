## The core truth

- **The moon is not a moon.** It is a living thing that came in from the dark about ten thousand years ago. It was small then, drifting and possibly wounded.
- **It was put to sleep.** The civilization that became Halcyon sang it into hibernation and held it in the sky.
- **Its shell is debris.** Over ten millennia it turned and slept while dust, ice and stray stone settled on it, layer on layer, until it looked like a moon.
- **Its name is a description.** The world calls it **the Pearl** and believes the name is only poetry. It is a literal account of how it formed.
- **The model** is a dormant living body wearing a shell of rock: something alive that the sky disguised.
- **It is a force of nature, not a character.** It never speaks, bargains or explains itself. It sleeps, stirs, draws and sheds the way a storm or a tide does. Every motive the player sees belongs to people. No text gives the Pearl lines or a point of view.
- **No one knows what it is for, and the story never says.** It never arrived, so no one has seen what it does on arrival. It might be a predator, a protector, a seed, or something with no human word. Every character's certainty is a guess:
  - the Choir calls it Mother and God;
  - Halcyon's engineers called it a resource;
  - the Bloom-touched call it a gardener;
  - the blanked are taken as proof that it is a predator.
  
  The bible fixes what happened to it, never what it is for, and no text may settle the question.
- **Closing line:** *"Every pearl begins with something that got inside, and something that couldn't get it out."*

## The history: arrival, freezing, feeding, fall, forgetting

1. **The arrival (about 10,000 years ago).** The Pearl came down toward the world to do whatever its kind does. No one alive then knew what that was, and no one has learned since.
2. **The freezing.** Halcyon's ancestors stopped it in the last moment before landfall. Thousands sang the first Lullaby at once, froze the arrival mid-act, and held the Pearl asleep in the sky. Whatever it came to do never happened. It was only paused. The singers acted out of fear of the unknown, not knowledge, and no one has ever been sure whether they saved the world or stopped something that would have saved it.
3. **The feeding.** Halcyon learned to draw on the frozen Pearl's light and built a civilization on it, with towers, Anchors, the Still and the Arks. Over millennia the light ran so deep in them that it became part of them, woven into every life. Whatever the Pearl is, a part of it now lives in everyone.
4. **The fall: the Undertow (1,200 years ago, owner-confirmed).** The heroes went into the Still around this time and slept for about twelve centuries.
   - **The cause:** the Pearl's pull was already growing, and Halcyon tried to escape it. Lifting the Arks took more light than had ever been drawn at once. The spike broke the Pearl's sleep for a single night.
   - **The night:** the coupling ran backwards and light surged home up every thread. Most of Halcyon was blanked by dawn. The tides rose higher than towers and threw the Arks inland, where they lie in the Arkgrave. The Anchors cracked, and the city fell.
5. **The forgetting.**
   - **The singers:** a remnant kept singing through the Undertow and put the Pearl back under. They became the Choir.
   - **The dark archives:** Halcyon kept its records in moonlight-powered archives, and when the city went dark its memory went dark with it.
   - **What survived:** chants, half-understood rites, and a moon to worship.
   - **What the Choir buried:** the rest, out of shame and out of fear of what people would do if they learned the thing in the sky is alive and inside them.

Over twelve centuries the Pearl's true origin became myth, then nothing. The world now believes the moon has always been there.

## The forgotten clue

The record has a seam. No painting, carving, song or star chart older than about ten thousand years shows a moon:

- **Before the seam:** old night skies are drawn empty, and the oldest shoreline ruins have no tide line, because there were no tides.
- **At the seam:** in a single layer of the record the Pearl is suddenly everywhere, in poetry, in calendars, and carved over doorways.
- **What changes in the figures:** before the seam, carved people are drawn flat and plain. After it, they are drawn with a glow at the chest, the first images of the inner light. People did not always carry the Pearl's light. It began when the Pearl came.

The party gathers this as relics across the campaign. The world has forgotten it, and the Choir does not want it remembered.

## The open mystery: predator, protector, or other

The evidence is real, but it never settles the question:
- **For predator:** the blanking, and the red hellspawn.
- **For protector or gardener:** the Bloom, life flourishing where its substance falls.
- **The debris itself:** for ten thousand years it has gathered stray stone out of the sky that might otherwise have fallen on the world. Was it catching that stone, or only collecting it?
- **What the blanking proves:** nothing. It is what happens when a stirring body draws back its own light, whatever that body is for.

Characters argue the question, factions are built on their answers, and the ending is a choice made without knowing. Writers keep the mystery open, including in the endings.
