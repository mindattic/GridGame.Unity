## The campaign arc, in the game's chapter order

The Pearl is drawn larger in each region's backdrops as the chapters go on. The story follows that approach.

| Chapter | Region | Discovery |
|---|---|---|
| Prologue | The Tithe Moors | The heroes wake from the Still into a world paying the Choir a tithe of people. |
| 1 | The Lantern Road | A moonless mural in a shrine raises the first question: why does the oldest art have no moon? |
| 2 | The Arkgrave | Under the wrecks is a harbour with no tide line. Halcyon tried to flee, and something made the seas rise. |
| 3 | The Anchor Wastes | The arches are taps, not supports. The draw is the pull. |
| 4 | Halcyon | Ruins and records show that Halcyon caught the Pearl and bound it through its own people. The light in everyone is the Pearl's. |
| 5 | The Bloom | The Pearl's life takes root here. It is not only a monster. |
| 6 | The Underspire | The active drain: someone is still pulling. |
| 7 | The Shardfall | The shell breaks and the body shows through. It is waking, and the blanking is it calling its light home. |
| 8 | Morrow's Shadow | Under the Pearl, gravity fails and stone rises. There is no one to reason with, only the weight of it. |

- **Threaded locations:** the Still and the Choir's Cathedral run through the arc. The Still is the heroes' origin. The Cathedral is the Choir's seat and the heart of the Lullaby, where its surplus is revealed.
- **Morrow's Shadow:** "morrow" means tomorrow, and the region keeps its name as the place where tomorrow's shadow falls. It could also be renamed The Pearl's Shadow (owner to decide).
- **Postgame:** the postgame regions (Free Holds, Drowned Arks, Deep Shardfall, Under the Anchors) return to the world after the ending and must agree with the ending chosen.

## Endings

Because the light is shared, every ending costs everyone:

- **Keep the Lullaby.** Side with the Choir: the Pearl sleeps and the world keeps its light, paid for in people. You may end the Choir's skim, which slows the decline, or leave it, and the sleep keeps thinning.
- **Cut the lines.** Sever every tap and the pull stops. The Pearl drifts back up. Without the circuit, people's borrowed light dims to what it was before the seam, so lives become shorter, quieter and plainer, but wholly their own. The world learns to live under an empty sky, as before.
- **Wake it.** Break the shell and let the Pearl wake: something small, old and starved, caught ten thousand years ago, whose light has been living in strangers ever since. It will not bargain or forgive. It will do what waking things do. The choice is people's alone: whether to let the light go home and who must be ready when it does. The ending is the world after, shaped by how well people prepared, not by anything the Pearl decides.

## Open questions (owner to decide)

- Is there a fixed lead, or is the whole party the protagonist?
- What is the tone: hopeful, bleak, or bittersweet?
- Is one ending canon, or does the player choose?
- Does any Choir leader know the full truth, including the skim?
- Echo, a blanked figure, could join the party and gain colour as the story goes on. Their portrait is deliberately blank until then.
