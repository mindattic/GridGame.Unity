Family Wheel

Every hero ability on one board. Each class starts on the rim of its family's wedge, angled toward the neighbour it plays most like. Anything on the wheel can be learned; the further round from your start, the more it costs.

How the wheel prices a move

Pick a class (or click its diamond on the rim) to shade every ability by what it costs that class. Hover any wedge, diamond or node for details.
