**Mana** is the Manalith's energy, and it is also the life in every person. This entry covers that bond. The word is always **mana**, never "essence".

**The core law: the Manalith's mana and human life are the same thing.** Since the first Lullaby, every person's life has carried a thread of the Manalith's mana, and the Manalith's sleep has been fed by theirs. Neither is wholly itself any more.

**What it looks like.** Mana is the faint cyan glow that seeps between the Manalith's crystals, the glow of the Still's tanks and the Cathedral's pipes, and the faint glow in every heartbeat that healers and Choir priests call "the colour". It is a person's warmth, vividness and self. The Moon's light is only light; it feeds nothing and binds no one.

**How it happened.** A living thing is bound by life, so Halcyon bound the Manalith through its own people. The first Lullaby braided their mana into its sleep and its mana back into them, and everyone born since carries a thread of it.

**How it works.** People are the conductors. Mana flows down the Anchors' lines, through living bodies, and back up as their life. Drawing mana down a line also pulls along it: ten thousand years of drawing became a physical pull, and the Manalith comes closer. Engineers called it a coupling and priests call it sympathy; both are true. In play, Halcyon tech, Choir hymns, Underspire neon, a healer's touch and the party's orbs all run on mana.

**Magic.** Magic is mana made to act. A mage, a hymn or a weapon gathers mana dust, concentrates it and channels it into a phenomenon or a change in a living body: lightning, fire, ice, sleep, poison. Because it is the same mana as the life in everyone, a spell can reach into a person as easily as into the air. All of it comes from the Manalith; without the Manalith there would be no mana to gather and no magic in the world.

**What the bond means:**
- **If the Manalith dies,** the mana in everyone goes out with it.
- **If the Manalith wakes,** its mana flows back to it. That is the blanking.
- **If people stop feeding it,** it wakes.

Neither side can leave without breaking the other. That is the trap at the centre of the world, and the reason no ending is clean.
