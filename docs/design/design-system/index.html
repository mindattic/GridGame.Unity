Design System

How every GridGame screen looks and behaves (GG-T2044): the owner's nine rules, the Manapunk tokens from Tokens.cs and Theme.uss, every kit component with its default, pressed, selected-pressed and disabled states, sheets and modals, and the layout grid and thumb zone for each device shape in tools/devices.json. Mocks are drawn at one third scale, so 132 design px is 44 CSS px. The written spec is DESIGN_SYSTEM.md.

1,250 G · gold is currency
Display · Cinzel, wordmark only
30 px: the floor. Nothing smaller.

One size, TypeScale.Base: GuiType picks the design size that draws 14 dp on this screen, clamped to 40 to 56, times Text size (100 / 115 / 130 %). Emphasis is weight and colour, never size. Shrink-to-fit stops at TypeFloor.ShrinkFloor: 30 px, or more on small or low-dpi screens (12 screen px / 9 dp). Wrapped lines stop at 720 px (--measure-text).

Fonts: M PLUS Rounded 1c Bold (Body), its ExtraBold (Heavy), Cinzel (Display). Three roles at most per screen; no faux bold.

Motion

Tap the track to play. Easings shown with the standard duration. Press feel: sink 0.06 s, release 0.14 s with overshoot. Screen change: 0.2 s fade through black. Reduce Motion drops shakes, wobble, flicker and marquee scroll.

Sheets and modals

All sit on GameApp.I.Overlay over a 0.72 veil kept topmost; Back closes the top one. A sheet rises in 0.25 s (Out) and drops in 0.15 s; a dialog fades and scales from 96 % in 0.15 s (Back).

Layout grid and thumb zone

One content column, 24-px gutters, a 4-column grid inside. Primary actions in the bottom 45 % of the safe stage (POL-C4). Pick a shape to draw it; "132 px is" converts the touch target into that phone's own pt or dp.
