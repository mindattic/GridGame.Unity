**Status: CANON.** The owner adopted it on 2026-11-21, and it replaces every earlier GridGame premise, including "A War in Heaven" and the colour/moon outline. It was read out of the art: the 213 portraits and 81 region backdrops. Every story text (crawls, conversations, lore, quests, barks, glossary) must agree with it.

**In one line:** the moon is alive. Everyone calls it **the Somnolith**, and its light is also the life in every person. It is coming down. The Choir keeps it asleep with **the Lullaby**, a rite that takes people's life to quiet it and keeps a share for the Choir. The heroes are sleepers woken from a failed vault, led by the First Sleeper, the last people whose light is wholly their own, crossing a world that has half given up. The tone is bittersweet, and the player chooses the ending.

**The name is the clue.** *Somnolith* is an old word built from *somnus* (sleep) and *lithos* (stone). Everyone takes it as poetry for a pale, still moon, "the sleeping stone". It is a literal description: a sleeper inside stone. The world has been saying the answer for ten thousand years.
